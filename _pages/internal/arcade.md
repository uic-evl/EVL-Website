The ARCADE is EVL's visual data science project room in the Computer Design Research and Learning Center (CDRLC), room 1219. It is built around a Sony LED wall of 14 by 7 cabinets, 6720 by 1890 pixels, capable of stereo 3D, driven by two rendering PCs: a collaboration PC for SAGE3 and Zoom, and a VR PC for stereo content. The room also has two 100-inch TVs with Mac Minis and AirPlay, projectors, ceiling microphones and speakers for video conferencing, and touch panels on each side of the wall to control inputs, cameras and sound.

## The guide

Everything about the room is in the user guide: the inventory, how to reserve the space, how to log in to the rendering PCs, the touch-panel controls, the cameras, the TVs, the 3D glasses, and where the supplies are.
