Welcome to EVL. This page covers the practical things: how to get set up, how the space works, and who to tell when something goes wrong.

## First week

- **Fill out the [EVL new person form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/vVjEmwzSuz).** This is how you get added to the lab's lists, accounts and the people page.
- **Get building and lab access.** Ask your advisor or the lab's staff to get your i-card activated for the lab doors.
- **Ask to be added to the lab's communication channels**, and watch for the weekly Tech Meeting announcement.
- **Login to Slack** at [evlers.slack.com](https://evlers.slack.com). Most day-to-day lab communication happens there.
- **Find a desk.** Talk to your advisor before claiming one; some desks belong to particular projects or groups.

## The space is shared

Almost everything at EVL is shared: the displays, the machines, the conference rooms, the kitchen, the tools and the couches. That works when people ask first and say what they are doing.

- **Ask before you take over a shared resource**, like a display wall, a VR system or a lab machine. If you need it for a stretch of time, tell people in advance.
- **Put things back where you found them.** Cables, adapters, controllers, chairs and tools belong to the lab, not to a desk.
- **Don't move or reconfigure shared equipment** without checking with the person or group responsible for it.
- **Guests and tours happen often.** Keep your area presentable, and if you are asked to show your work, it helps everyone.

## When something breaks

**Stuff breaks. That's okay, and you will not be in trouble for it.** What causes problems is nobody knowing.

- **Tell a faculty member or the lab staff as soon as you notice**, even if you don't think you caused it, and even if it still mostly works.
- **Say what happened** so the next person doesn't repeat it, and so it can be fixed properly.
- **Don't quietly swap in a replacement part, re-cable a system, or "fix" it yourself** unless it is yours to maintain.
- If something is **unsafe**, like a damaged power cable or a leak, unplug it if you can do so safely, then tell someone immediately.

## The kitchen

The kitchen is a shared space. Be mindful when using appliances, the fridge, and the garbage.

- **Don't leave food in the fridge for long.** If it is yours and it's old, throw it out.
- **If the garbage is full**, take the bag to the hallway for the janitors and put a fresh bag in the bin. Spare bags are in the lab.
- **Clean up after you eat.** That means the counters, the table and the couches.
- **Cleaning supplies are under the sink**, and there is a vacuum and a mop if you need them.

Keeping the kitchen clean keeps critters out of the lab, and keeps it a decent place for everyone who works, studies and hangs out here.

## Being a good lab citizen

- **Come to Tech Meetings.** It is how the lab keeps up with what everyone is doing.
- **Credit the lab in your work.** Use the lab's acknowledgment text on papers and talks, and check with your advisor about funding acknowledgments.
- **Headphones for audio**, and take long calls to a conference room or the hallway.
- **Last one out**, check the doors and turn off the equipment you turned on.
- **If you are unsure about anything**, ask. Nobody expects a new person to know how the space works.
