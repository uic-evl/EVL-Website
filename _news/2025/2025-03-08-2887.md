## Immersive Analytics at the Electronic Visualization Laboratory

September 2023 marked the 50th anniversary of the Electronic Visualization Laboratory (EVL). This paper summarizes EVL&rsquo;s efforts in Visual Data Science, with a focus on the many networked, immersive, collaborative visualization and virtual reality (VR) systems and applications the Lab has developed and deployed, aswell as lessons learned and future plans.<br><br><strong>Index terms:</strong> Visualization, Virtual Reality, Visual Data Science, Collaboration
