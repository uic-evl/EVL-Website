**Ilamathy, S.,Joy, R. A.,Dearing, M.T.,Lan, Z.**

- Link: [https://gcasr.org/2025/posters](https://gcasr.org/2025/posters)

<strong>Motivation:</strong><br>•Parallel Discrete Event Simulations (PDES) offers accurate HPC simulations but is computationally intensive and slow to scale.<br>•Surrogate models can accelerate simulations, and we explore if causal insights can improve their long-term forecasting stability.<br><br><strong>Research Questions:</strong><br>•Can Causal Signals hidden in HPC simulations unlock better forecasting?<br>•Can different Causal Discovery methods identify key drivers for accurate surrogate forecasting?<br>
