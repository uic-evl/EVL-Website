## Supporting Transoceanic Collaborations in Virtual Environment
**Imai, T.,Johnson, A.,Leigh, J.,Pape, D.,DeFanti, T.**
- Location: Beijing, China
- PDF: [tansoceanic.pdf](/documents/tansoceanic.pdf)

[![image](/images/vmail.jpg-srcw.jpg){:style="max-width: 100%"}](/images/vmail.jpg-srcw.jpg)
- Credit: T. Imai

In this paper we present a virtual mail (v-mail) system. The prototype system is developed to realize efficent &ldquo;handing-off&rdquo; of work between participants that are separated by great distances and/or time zones.

**Citation**: Imai, T., Johnson, A., Leigh, J., Pape, D., DeFanti, T., Supporting Transoceanic Collaborations in Virtual Environment, *Proceedings of the 5th Asia-Pacific Conference on Communications / 4th Optoelectronics and Communications Conference - APCC / OECC &rsquo;99*, Beijing, China, pp. 1059-1062, 1999-10-19.
