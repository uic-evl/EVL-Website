## A Genetic Programming Application in Virtual Reality
**Das, S.,Franguiadakis, T.,Papka, M,DeFanti, T.,Sandin, D.**
- Location: IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence

[![image](/images/genetic.gif){:style="max-width: 100%"}](/images/genetic.gif)
- Caption: Evolution of Behavior in a Simulated Environment
- Credit: T. Franguiadakis, EVL

This paper describes a CAVE application, Evolution of Behavior in a Simulated Environment. The application uses genetic programming techniques to evolve avoidance and goal getting behaviors. Results are shown as real-time, 3-dimensional, stereo forms.

**Citation**: Das, S., Franguiadakis, T., Papka, M, DeFanti, T., Sandin, D., A Genetic Programming Application in Virtual Reality, *Proceedings of the First IEEE Conference on Evolutionary Computing (ICEC &rsquo;94)*, vol 1, IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence, pp. 480-484, 1994-06-01.
