ACM SIGGRAPH has published a Member Profile of <strong>Dana Plepys</strong>, who recently retired after 36 years as Associate Director of the Electronic Visualization Laboratory (EVL) at the University of Illinois Chicago.<br><br>
In the profile, Plepys reflects on a career spanning lab operations, proposal development, project management, and technology transfer, and on her longstanding role in supporting EVL&rsquo;s students, researchers, and faculty. She also looks back on more than four decades with SIGGRAPH, beginning as a student volunteer at SIGGRAPH &rsquo;83 and continuing through 31 years producing the SIGGRAPH Video Review, a 180-plus-issue archive of computer graphics history now digitized and preserved with the Charles Babbage Institute.<br><br>
Plepys holds a BFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and an MFA in Electronic Visualization from UIC.
