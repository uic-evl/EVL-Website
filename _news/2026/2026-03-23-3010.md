&ldquo;Advancing Normal Tissue Complication Probability Modeling with Supervised Contrastive Learning for Predicting Osteoradionecrosis&rdquo; by a multidisciplinary team led by Prof. Canahuate at the University of Iowa and including UIC CS Prof. Zhang and Prof. Marai has received a Best Paper Award at the ACM Symposium on Applied Computing (SAC) 2026 in Thessaloniki, Greece. The paper describes the development of a supervised contrastive learning model for predicting a delayed-onset, significant toxicity associated with radiation therapy treatment for head and neck cancer.<br><br>
