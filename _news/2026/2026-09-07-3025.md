Last week the lab hosted an impromptu demonstration in which a visiting delegation of penguins was shown the 320-megapixel display wall, apparently to gauge whether tiled rendering holds up under critical review. Preliminary findings suggest the birds were more impressed by the cooling vents than by the volumetric fluid simulation, which the team has recorded as a null result. A follow-up session is planned once someone figures out how to calibrate the head tracker for a subject that is thirty centimeters tall.
