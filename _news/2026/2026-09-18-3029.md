EVL Professor **G. Elisabeta (Liz) Marai** has been awarded an NIH R01 from the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR) for *SCH: Flavor Charter: Precision Data-Driven Diagnosis, Monitoring and Guidance for Dysgeusia*. Co-investigators are Hananeh Esmailbeigi (Biomedical Engineering), Ameen Salahudeen (Department of Medicine, College of Medicine), and **Xinhua Zhang** (Computer Science). The project includes a subaward to the University of Iowa, where Guadalupe Canahuate serves as subaward PI.

Taste loss (dysgeusia, or altered taste) is a significant toxicity in head and neck cancer patients that changes their quality of life. It affects 51–100% of patients at the end of radiotherapy and 23–50% of patients one to two years after treatment, with no known end date. The poor nutritional choices that can follow may contribute to sarcopenia, or muscle wasting, which multiple datasets directly associate with increased mortality and poorer clinical outcomes.

Flavor Charter creates a data-driven computational approach to address critical gaps in how dysgeusia is diagnosed and monitored, and to create and deliver nutritional guidance for patients living with it.

Congratulations to Liz and the team!
