EVL Director <strong>Michael E. Papka</strong> delivered a keynote at <a href="https://esto.nasa.gov/estim2026/">ESTIM 2026</a>, the Earth Science Technology Interchange Meeting organized by NASA&rsquo;s Earth Science Technology Office (ESTO), held September 22&ndash;24, 2026 at The Mart in Chicago. His talk, &ldquo;Fifty Years at the Electronic Visualization Laboratory,&rdquo; traced EVL&rsquo;s history from its founding to today and presented some lessons learned along the way to an audience of NASA Earth science and technology researchers.
